Linecheck

Privacy policy · Effective October 27, 2026

Short version: Linecheck has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and never sells your data. Most data stays on your own device. If you create an account, only what you need to sync is stored on our server.

What stays on your device

Your tickets, bet tracker, settings, color theme and cached sports data are saved in your browser's storage on your phone or computer. We can't see them unless you sign in to an account.

If you create an account

  • Username you choose. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Password: your phone scrambles it before sending, and our server scrambles it again before storing. We never see or store your actual password. The same applies to your recovery code.
  • Synced data: your current ticket, saved tickets, bet tracker entries, color theme, tile layout, alert and filter settings, so they match on all your devices.
  • Sign-in sessions: a random token kept on your device; the server keeps only a scrambled copy, which expires after 180 days.

If you turn on alerts

When you allow notifications, your browser gives us a push address for your device. We store it with the picks on your saved tickets (game, team, line) so our server can tell you when a whole ticket wins. Turning alerts off removes it.

Where it's stored

Account and alert data is stored with Cloudflare, which runs our server. Cloudflare may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the service.

Other services the app talks to

  • ESPN: the app loads scores, schedules, stats and odds directly from ESPN's public feeds, so ESPN receives normal web requests from your device.
  • Google Fonts: fonts may load from Google.
  • Sportsbook links: buttons that open DraftKings take you to DraftKings' own app or site, under their privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, ad tracking or analytics tools.
  • No selling or sharing your data for marketing.
  • No location tracking, contacts or photos access.
  • We never take bets, payments or hold money.

Deleting your data

Delete your account any time in the app: tap the person button, then Delete my account. This removes your username, password and synced data from our server right away. To clear data on a device, clear the site's data in your browser settings or uninstall the app.

Children

Linecheck is only for adults 21 and older (or the legal betting age where you live). We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top and show a note in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the support contact on Linecheck's App Store or Google Play listing.
